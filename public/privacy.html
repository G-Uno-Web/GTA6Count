1. 适用范围

2. 谁在运营

本站是个人自建的小工具集合，不是公司，也没有编辑部。就个人数据保护而言，运营者即本政策的责任方；所有与隐私相关的事宜都可直接联系（见第 10 节）。站点不含账号体系，因此我们无法、也不会通过任何登录信息识别你。

3. 我们收集什么，不收集什么

不收集的内容：

  • 没有注册、登录、评论、留言板，不收集姓名、邮箱、手机号、身份证件；
  • 没有订阅列表、没有营销邮件、没有短信；
  • 没有支付环节，不接触任何银行卡或账单信息；
  • 不使用第三方流量分析脚本（如 Google Analytics、Meta Pixel），也不做跨站用户画像；
  • 工具中打开的文件（UnlockMusic 的音乐文件等）只在你的浏览器内解密，不会上传——站点没有能接收这些文件的服务端。

会产生的数据：

5. Cookie 与同类技术

本站自身不设置任何 Cookie：没有会话、没有登录态、没有自建统计。页面上出现的 Cookie 全部来自第三方广告服务（Google AdSense），由 Google 在其域下写入，我们无法读取其内容。

你可以在浏览器设置中随时删除或阻止 Cookie。阻止后页面主体功能不受影响，但广告可能不再个性化，或出现重复展示。

6. 广告与 Google AdSense

本站通过 Google AdSense 展示广告。Google 作为第三方供应商，会使用 Cookie 根据你此前对本站或其他网站的访问投放广告。

  • Google 使用广告 Cookie，使其及其合作伙伴能基于你的访问向你展示广告；
  • 可在 Google 广告设置 关闭个性化广告；
  • 也可在 aboutads.info 或 Your Online Choices 统一管理第三方广告 Cookie；
  • 关闭个性化后仍会看到广告，只是不再基于你的浏览行为；
  • Google 如何使用这些数据：合作伙伴网站说明、广告技术说明、Google 隐私权政策。

广告由 Google 直接投放、直接计费，我们不掌握点击数据、不接触广告主，也不会把任何信息卖给第三方——站点没有可卖的数据。

若你位于欧洲经济区（EEA）、英国或瑞士，Google 会先依据当地法规征求你的同意，再决定是否投放个性化广告；你可以随时通过页面上的同意提示或上述设置更改选择。

7. 服务器日志与保留

本站托管在 Cloudflare，请求会经过其全球边缘节点。Cloudflare 会自动记录常规技术日志（IP 地址、User-Agent、请求路径、时间戳、来源页），用于 DDoS 防护、限流和故障排查，保留期限取决于 Cloudflare 的政策与我们后台可查的时间范围。我们不会把这些日志与任何个人身份关联，也不会用于广告定向。

由于日志位于 Cloudflare 的全球网络，你的请求可能由你所在地区附近的节点处理，这一过程可能涉及跨境传输。

8. 第三方链接与外部服务

站内会链接到外部资源，例如 Google、GitHub、游戏官方公告、公开油价数据来源等。这些网站有自己的隐私政策与 Cookie 行为，我们无法控制，也不对其内容或数据处理负责；离开本站后请以对方页面为准。

通过 GitHub Issues 反馈问题时，你提交的内容（包括 GitHub 账号、文字、截图）受 GitHub 的服务条款与隐私政策约束，且是公开可见的——请不要在公开 issue 里填写邮箱、订单号等个人信息。

9. 你的权利

依据 GDPR、UK GDPR、CCPA 等法规，你可能拥有访问、更正、删除、限制处理、反对处理以及数据可携带的权利。就本站而言：

  • 我们没有你的账号与身份信息，通常没有可供导出或删除的个人数据；
  • 本地偏好由你自行清除浏览器数据即可删除，无需向我们申请；
  • 广告相关的数据由 Google 控制，需通过上文链接在 Google 侧行使权利；
  • 若你认为某项处理不当，可来信说明，我们会核查并在合理期限内答复；你也可以向所在地的数据保护机构投诉。
本站不以 13 岁以下儿童为目标受众，也不会主动收集儿童的个人信息。若你是家长并认为孩子向我们提交了信息，请联系我们处理。

Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: 2026-11-12. This policy applies to g-uno.top and every page and tool on its subdomains — the price lookup, the game checklist, the unlock countdown, the music tool, the slang dictionary, and these informational pages.

1. Who runs this site

This is a personal collection of small browser tools, not a company. There are no accounts, so we cannot identify you through any login.

2. What we do not collect

  • No sign-up, login or comments — no names, email addresses or phone numbers;
  • No mailing list, no marketing email, no SMS;
  • No payments, so no card or billing details;
  • No third-party analytics scripts (no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel) and no cross-site profiling;
  • Files you open in tools such as UnlockMusic are decrypted locally in your browser and never uploaded — there is no server that could receive them.

3. What is generated

Type Source Purpose
Technical access logs (IP, user agent, path, timestamp, referrer) Cloudflare edge, automatically Security, rate limiting, debugging, coarse traffic counts
Local preferences Your browser's localStorage Remember checklist progress and reference-clock choice
Advertising cookies Google AdSense Ad delivery and frequency capping

4. Data stored in your browser

Two tools keep small preferences in localStorage: the game checklist stores which items you have ticked, and the countdown page remembers whether you prefer your own clock, UTC or Beijing time. This stays on your device and is not readable by us; clearing site data removes it.

5. Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own — no sessions, no login state, no self-hosted analytics. Any cookies you encounter come from Google's advertising service and are written on Google's domains; we cannot read them. Blocking cookies does not break the tools, though ads may become non-personalised or repeat.

6. Advertising and Google AdSense

Ads are served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Opt out of personalised advertising via Google Ads Settings;
  • Manage third-party ad cookies at aboutads.info or Your Online Choices;
  • Opting out does not remove ads, only their personalisation;
  • How Google uses this data: partner sites notice, ad technologies, Google Privacy Policy.

Ads are served and billed by Google directly. We never see click-level data, have no relationship with advertisers, and sell nothing — there is no data here to sell.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google asks for your consent before serving personalised ads, in line with local law. You can change that choice at any time via the consent message or the settings above.

7. Server logs and retention

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which keeps standard technical logs for DDoS mitigation, rate limiting and troubleshooting, retained under its own policy. We do not link these logs to any identity and do not use them for ad targeting. Because Cloudflare's network is global, requests may be handled by an edge node near you, which can involve cross-border transfer.

8. Third-party links

Pages link to external resources such as Google, GitHub, official game announcements and public fuel-price data. Those sites have their own policies and we are not responsible for them. Feedback submitted through GitHub Issues is governed by GitHub's terms and is publicly visible — please do not post personal details there.

9. Your rights

  • We hold no account or identity data, so there is usually nothing to export or delete;
  • Local preferences are removed by clearing your browser data — no request needed;
  • Advertising data is controlled by Google; exercise those rights through the links above;
  • If you believe something is handled incorrectly, write to us and we will look into it; you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Contact

Privacy, copyright or advertising questions: happygc913@gmail.com. Public issues and suggestions: GitHub Issues.
