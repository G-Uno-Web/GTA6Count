FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What time can I actually play, and why is it different everywhere?

GTA VI releases November 19, 2026, and Rockstar unlocks the game at 00:00 local time in each region. New Zealand, being closest to the international date line, hits midnight first — 11:00 UTC on November 18. Hong Kong, China, Japan and Singapore follow, and the US comes last at 05:00 UTC on November 19. That is an 18-hour spread, which is why the "earliest unlock zone" gets so much attention every launch.

Is there a PC version? Can I stream it early?

Not at launch. Only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S are confirmed, with no PC date or system requirements — and no PC build exists to stream. Services like GeForce NOW can only stream a PC version that actually exists, and Xbox has denied any PC-exclusive streaming arrangement. GTA V took 19 months to reach PC and Red Dead Redemption 2 took 13, so analysts expect a PC version in 2027, but Rockstar has not confirmed anything.

When does preload start? What if 210GB will not fit?

Digital preload opens at 00:00 local time on November 12. The files are encrypted and unplayable until the 19th. The PS5 version is about 210GB; a PS5 Slim has roughly 840GB usable, leaving around 630GB afterwards — before the day-one patch. Clear out games you no longer play or install an M.2 SSD before the 12th; leave it until the evening of the 18th and you will be stuck behind everyone else in the download queue. Physical editions contain a download code, not a disc.

Standard vs Ultimate edition, and pre-order bonuses?

US digital pricing is $79.99 for Standard and $99.99 for Ultimate, with pre-orders including one month of GTA+ among other bonuses. Regional pricing and bonuses vary, so check your own store page before buying.
